What we treat

Care built around how teeth actually misbehave.

From routine cleanings to the visits nobody wants to book — we cover the full range, without the referral runaround.

Cleanings & checkups

Twice-a-year visits with a hygienist who actually remembers your name and your last filling.

Invisalign & braces

Clear-aligner plans mapped out visually before you commit, with monthly progress check-ins.

Same-day crowns

Milled in-studio while you wait — one visit instead of the usual two-appointment wait.

Root canals

Handled gently, with clear pain-management options explained before we start, not after.

Implants & bridges

Full-arch and single-tooth restorations planned with 3D imaging, not guesswork.

Cosmetic whitening

In-studio or take-home kits, calibrated to your natural shade rather than a generic max-white.

Your first visit

Four steps, one afternoon, zero surprises.

Every new patient moves through the same short sequence — so you always know what's coming next.

01 — Intake

Tell us what's going on

A short form and a real conversation about symptoms, history, and what "healthy teeth" means to you.

02 — Imaging

Digital X-rays, low dose

Full-mouth digital scans, reviewed on screen with you — not filed away for later.

03 — The plan

See the options, in writing

Costs, timelines, and alternatives laid out before any drill touches anything.

04 — Treatment

Book only what you approve

We start with the first agreed step — no bundled add-ons, no pressure in the chair.
